Frequently Asked Questions

How do I roll dice?

Set the number of each die type in the Dice section, then tap Roll.

Can I roll by shaking my phone?

Yes — enable "Roll on device shake" in Settings. You may be prompted to allow motion access.

How do I change the look of the dice?

Open Settings → Dice Styles to choose a color and material.

Why are roll results not showing?

Enable "Show result in toasts" in Settings → Toasts.

How do I reset everything?

Tap "Back to Defaults" at the bottom of the Settings section.

How many dice can I roll at once?

Up to 9 dice per type — for example 9d6 + 9d20.

Is any data collected about me?

No. The app stores your settings and roll history exclusively on your device. No data ever leaves it. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What does Premium unlock?

A one-time purchase (not a subscription) unlocks Decks, Roll History, dice/tray styling, shake-to-roll physics, and toast notifications. Basic dice rolling stays free forever.
